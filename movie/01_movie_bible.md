# 01 — Movie Bible

## Working genre
Investigation thriller / murder mystery / family drama.

## Language
Tamil.

## Audience
Tamil theatrical / OTT audience.

## Setting
Contemporary Tamil Nadu, India. The central family world is built around the estate and the entertainment empire of Nagarajan.

## Central protagonist / investigator
Aditya Karikalan — the principal investigative character. He should be intelligent, observant, restrained, and deduction-driven rather than an action hero.

## Central patriarch
Nagarajan — veteran superstar actor and producer who built a powerful entertainment empire over several decades. His legacy, assets, relationships, and succession decision drive the family conflict.

## Core mystery
Nagarajan dies under suspicious circumstances. The family has overlapping motives, secrets, conflicting accounts, and incomplete knowledge. The investigation must progressively reconstruct what happened.

## Tone
Intelligent, tense, atmospheric, emotionally restrained. Suspense should arise from behaviour, contradictions, objects, silence, timing, and deductions rather than constant action.

## Visual language
Photorealistic premium Tamil cinema; authentic Tamil Nadu architecture and environments; realistic costumes and vehicles; practical/natural lighting; controlled camera movement; motivated dolly/tracking/crane/handheld shots; restrained film grain; realistic skin and physics.

## Story rules
1. Mystery information is controlled deliberately.
2. Every important clue has a purpose and a later interpretation.
3. Red herrings must be plausible but must not invalidate the final solution.
4. Character knowledge and audience knowledge must be tracked separately.
5. Timeline and physical geography must remain internally consistent.
6. Do not introduce a major solution element after the fact without foreshadowing.
7. The family drama must feel culturally credible in a Tamil context.
8. Avoid caricatured Tamil dialogue; use natural contemporary spoken Tamil.

## Source adaptation rule
The uploaded Hollywood screenplay is a reference source. The Tamil film should be an original adaptation: retain only permissible high-level concepts/ideas while creating original characters, dialogue, scenes, staging, visual expression, and Tamil cultural context. Do not reproduce the source screenplay verbatim.
