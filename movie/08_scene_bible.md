# 08 — 80-Scene Story Bible

## Structure
The intended feature is an 80-scene Tamil investigation thriller.

### Act 1 — Death and family setup
Scenes 01–20

### Act 2A — Investigation and suspects
Scenes 21–40

### Act 2B — Hidden truth and reconstruction
Scenes 41–60

### Act 3 — Second crime / deeper reveal
Scenes 61–75

### Climax and final reconstruction
Scenes 76–80

## Scene 01 locked visual concept
Morning at Nagarajan Estate. The estate is grand, Tamil Nadu-inspired and believable for a veteran superstar/producer. Bright natural daylight. Peaceful and elegant atmosphere with subtle mystery. Establishing visuals can show remnants of a recent celebration. The drinking vessel is a modern drinks glass, not a tea glass.

## Development status
The full 80-scene story should be treated as editable until the user approves individual scenes. Do not claim all 80 scenes are already locked if they have not been explicitly created and approved in this project state.

## Scene command contract
When the user says `Generate Scene XX`, load all authoritative project context and generate the complete available production pipeline for that scene, then present it for review. Do not mark it final until the user explicitly approves.
