# 03 — Location Bible

## Nagarajan Estate
Grand Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury estate belonging to Nagarajan. It should feel like the residence of a veteran superstar/producer who built wealth over decades, combining South Indian/Tamil architectural influence with understated old-money luxury.

### Locked Scene 01 appearance
- Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury estate
- Grand but believable
- Bright natural morning daylight
- Peaceful, elegant atmosphere with subtle mystery
- Photorealistic cinematic Tamil-film visual language
- Celebration remnants may include tasteful decorations, chairs, tables, glasses and other believable party objects
- The corrected drinks glass is a modern drinks glass, not a tea glass

### Continuity
Architecture, entrance geometry, windows, corridors, stairs, major furniture and recurring props must remain consistent across scenes.

## Main Hall
A large central gathering space displaying Nagarajan's legacy: film posters, awards, framed photographs, production stills and family photographs. It should communicate that Nagarajan built his empire rather than inherited it.

## Study / Library
Nagarajan's private working space. Used for confidential conversations, documents, business decisions and investigation clues.

## Bedroom
Nagarajan's private bedroom and adjacent spaces. Household routines, medication/health-related props and personal objects may appear only when supported by the approved story.

## Estate exterior
Driveway, gate/security area, garden and upper-floor windows are recurring geography. The physical route between these areas must remain fixed for timeline reconstruction.

## Police / investigation locations
Inspector Stephen's procedural investigation spaces should be realistic Tamil Nadu police environments, unless a later approved location is specified.

## Location generation rule
Do not change architecture, room proportions, window/door positions or recurring set dressing between shots without explicit approval.
