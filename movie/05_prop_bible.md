# 05 — Prop Bible

Props are continuity-critical in an investigation thriller.

## Scene 01 locked prop correction
The celebration-remnants drinking vessel is a modern drinks glass. It must not resemble a South Indian tea tumbler.

## Recurring prop categories
- Nagarajan's personal objects
- Family/estate objects
- Business and legacy documents
- Phones and communication devices
- Household objects
- Police evidence and documentation
- Any clue-bearing object introduced by the approved story

## Prop rule
Every mystery-critical prop receives a permanent PROP-ID, owner/location, first appearance, later appearances, apparent meaning, true meaning, and reveal scene. AI generation must preserve its appearance whenever it recurs.
