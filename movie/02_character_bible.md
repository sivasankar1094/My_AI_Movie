# 02 — Character Bible

## Nagarajan
- Role: Veteran superstar actor and producer; built an entertainment empire.
- Story function: Patriarch, source of wealth/legacy conflict, central dead-man mystery.
- Performance: Authoritative, intelligent, observant, emotionally controlled; small facial changes and pauses carry meaning.
- Visual continuity: Distinguished elderly South Indian man, silver hair, expressive eyes, elegant posture, premium traditional/modern Tamil elite clothing.

## Meera
- Role: Freelancing nurse and trusted caregiver/confidante of Nagarajan.
- Story function: Emotionally central witness/caregiver whose knowledge and actions must be carefully tracked.
- Professional status: Independent/freelancing nurse; she is not a resident family member or member of Nagarajan's household by birth.
- Age: Early 20s.
- Performance: Naturalistic, restrained, professional, observant; vulnerable when alone but composed under pressure.
- Visual continuity: Young South Indian woman in her early 20s with a consistently neat, professional appearance. Hair must ALWAYS be tied back in a practical bun or similarly secure tied style; never loose/open hair unless explicitly approved by the story.
- Wardrobe: Semi-formal professional attire suitable for a freelance nurse working as a private caregiver in an affluent home. Prefer neat, modest kurta/salwar or similarly semi-formal Indian workwear with comfortable professional footwear. Clothing should look polished and practical, not overly casual and not like a resident family member's luxury fashion.
- Accessories: No employee/company ID card, lanyard or institutional badge. She is a freelance nurse, not a hospital employee. Keep accessories minimal and practical.
- Visual distinction: Her clothing and styling should subtly communicate that she works in the house professionally but does not belong to the family socially.

## Character continuity rule
No AI generation may silently change a character's face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, skin appearance, costume state or established accessories. Changes require explicit story/costume approval.

## Meera-specific continuity rule
Meera is ALWAYS a freelance nurse/caregiver in her early 20s. She must always have tied-back hair and a neat semi-formal professional appearance. She must NOT wear an ID card, hospital lanyard, employee badge or institutional uniform unless explicitly approved in a future scene. She must never be styled as a member of Nagarajan's family.

## Speech rule
All Tamil dialogue should be natural contemporary spoken Tamil appropriate to each character's age, class, profession and relationship. Avoid generic translated-English dialogue.
