# 02 — Character Bible

## Nagarajan
- Role: Veteran superstar actor and producer; built an entertainment empire.
- Story function: Patriarch, source of wealth/legacy conflict, central dead-man mystery.
- Performance: Authoritative, intelligent, observant, emotionally controlled; small facial changes and pauses carry meaning.
- Visual continuity: Distinguished elderly South Indian man, silver hair, expressive eyes, elegant posture, premium traditional/modern Tamil elite clothing.

## Meera
- Role: Trusted caregiver/confidante of Nagarajan.
- Story function: Emotionally central witness/caregiver whose knowledge and actions must be carefully tracked.
- Performance: Naturalistic, restrained, vulnerable when alone but composed under pressure.
- Visual continuity: Lock age, face, hairstyle, costume state and accessories once finalized.

## Aditya Karikalan
- Role: Principal investigator/deduction-driven protagonist.
- Story function: Reconstructs the truth from contradictions and evidence.
- Performance: Calm, analytical, observant; avoids unnecessary theatrics.
- Visual continuity: Lock facial identity, hairstyle, body proportions, wardrobe and signature mannerisms.

## Arjun
- Role: Nagarajan's family member / important suspect.
- Story function: Strong emotional and inheritance-conflict axis; must remain potentially suspicious without premature confirmation.
- Performance: Controlled, sometimes defensive; reactions should carry subtext.

## Character continuity rule
No AI generation may silently change a character's face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, skin appearance, costume state or established accessories. Changes require explicit story/costume approval.

## Speech rule
All Tamil dialogue should be natural contemporary spoken Tamil appropriate to each character's age, class, profession and relationship. Avoid generic translated-English dialogue.
