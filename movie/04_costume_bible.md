# 04 — Costume Bible

Costumes remain provisional until scene-specific wardrobe is approved.

## Nagarajan
Premium Tamil elite clothing: elegant white/cream veshti and shirt, silk kurta or sophisticated contemporary Indian wear depending on scene. Avoid flashy costume unless story requires it.

## Meera
Practical caregiver/household wardrobe appropriate to her background and the time of day. Keep a locked outfit per continuous sequence.

## Aditya Karikalan
Smart, understated investigator wardrobe. Contemporary Indian/Tamil urban styling. Clothing should support movement and observation rather than action-hero styling.

## Arjun / Latha / Raghuvaran / Ashok Kumar / Niral
Wardrobe should communicate age, family position, profession and personality without becoming caricature. Lock outfit per sequence.

## Kamala / Indirani
Authentic Tamil household/family styling appropriate to age and role.

## Inspector Stephen
Realistic Tamil Nadu police uniform when on duty; realistic civilian clothing when off duty.

## Continuity rule
Once a costume is approved for a continuous timeline segment, it cannot change between shots unless an explicit costume-change event occurs.
