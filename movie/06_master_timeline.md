# 06 — Master Timeline

## Timeline rules
- Record story date/time for every scene.
- Record every character's location.
- Record costume state and significant props.
- Track who knows each fact at each point.
- Track what the audience knows separately from what characters know.
- Physical travel time and estate geography must be plausible.
- Flashbacks must be explicitly anchored to a known event/time.
