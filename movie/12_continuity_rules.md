# 12 — Continuity Rules

## Character
Never silently change face, age, hairstyle, body proportions, skin appearance or established mannerisms.

## Costume
Keep exact approved costume through continuous sequences. A costume change must be story/time motivated.

## Location
Keep room dimensions, doors, windows, stairs, entrances, exits and recurring set dressing fixed.

## Props
Mystery-critical props receive stable appearance and identity across all shots/scenes.

## Timeline
Every scene has an explicit time state. Flashbacks must be anchored.

## Knowledge
Track audience knowledge separately from each character's knowledge. No character may act on information they have not plausibly learned.

## Camera geography
Shot changes must respect physical positions and eyelines.

## Mystery
Every major clue should be foreshadowed before its final interpretation. Red herrings cannot break the eventual causal logic.

## Approval
Approved visual references are locked. User-requested changes supersede previous visual decisions only after explicit confirmation.
