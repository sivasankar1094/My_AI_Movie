# 11 — AI Prompt System

## Master scene command
`Generate Scene XX`

Interpret as: load Movie Bible + Character Bible + Location Bible + Costume Bible + Prop Bible + Timeline + Clue Database + approved previous continuity; generate scene screenplay; shot division; keyframe/reference prompts; video-generation prompts; Tamil voice/dialogue prompts; sound design; music cues; editing instructions; continuity/QC; then present a review package. Do not mark final until explicit user approval.

## Screenplay prompt requirements
- Original Tamil cinematic screenplay.
- Natural spoken Tamil.
- Character-specific speech patterns.
- Subtext.
- Minimal exposition.
- Visual storytelling.
- Maintain clue placement and knowledge states.

## Shot prompt requirements
For every shot specify ID, duration, shot size, camera position, lens, camera movement, composition, subject, character movement, expression, eye direction, blocking, foreground/background, lighting, focus, sound, dialogue, music, transition and continuity.

## Keyframe prompt requirements
Match locked character identity, age, face, hair, costume, location, architecture, props, lighting, time, camera angle, lens and composition.

## Video prompt requirements
Use the approved keyframe/reference. Specify exact camera and subject movement, facial performance, body movement, environmental motion, realistic cloth/hair/physics, start/end composition and continuity. Avoid face morphing, identity changes, extra limbs, duplicated people, floating objects, costume drift, background changes and random camera movement.

## Voice prompt requirements
Tamil language; character-specific age, pitch, pace, accent, emotion and subtext; natural cinematic acting; no assistant-like delivery.

## Sound prompt requirements
Dialogue, Foley, ambience, background layers, suspense elements, intentional silence, and perspective changes according to camera distance.

## Music prompt requirements
Original Tamil cinematic thriller score; scene emotion and story function; recurring motifs may be established for characters/mystery; never overpower dialogue.

## Editing prompt requirements
Define shot order, duration, cut points, J/L cuts, dialogue continuity, sound/music continuity, reaction shots, clue visibility and pacing.

## QC
Check character identity, costume, location, props, timeline, knowledge states, clues, blocking, camera geography, lighting, sound, music, transitions and AI consistency.
