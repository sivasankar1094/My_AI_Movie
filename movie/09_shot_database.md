# 09 — Shot Database

Every approved scene can be decomposed into production shots.

Each shot record should contain:
- SHOT-ID
- Scene
- Duration
- Shot size
- Camera position/height
- Lens
- Composition
- Camera movement
- Subject/action
- Facial performance
- Blocking
- Foreground/background
- Lighting
- Focus/depth of field
- Dialogue
- Sound
- Music
- Transition
- Continuity dependencies
- Reference image ID
- Video generation status
- QC status

## Shot-generation principle
Shots must form a physically and editorially coherent sequence. Avoid random camera movement, impossible geography, identity drift, costume changes, prop drift, and unexplained time jumps.
