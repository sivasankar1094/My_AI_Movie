# Scene 06 — Initial Investigation

**Status:** VISUAL REFERENCE PENDING USER REVIEW

## Scene purpose
The first formal police examination begins inside Nagarajan Estate. Inspector Stephen takes control, listens to Meera's account, questions Arjun about Nagarajan's routine, and notices a small inconsistency around the study window. The scene establishes Stephen as methodical rather than theatrical and introduces the idea that the death requires closer investigation.

## Location
Nagarajan Estate — Nagarajan's private study and adjoining bedroom/living area.

## Time
Bright morning, shortly after the discovery and initial household response.

## Characters
- Inspector Stephen
- Meera
- Arjun
- Forensic officers / police personnel

## Screenplay

**INT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — PRIVATE STUDY — MORNING**

The room is now controlled. Police personnel work quietly around the edges while the central area remains undisturbed.

Inspector Stephen enters and takes in the room before speaking.

He looks at Nagarajan, then at the desk, then toward the windows.

**STEPHEN**
"யாரும் எதையும் தொடக்கூடாது."

Meera stands nearby, visibly shaken but composed.

Stephen turns to her.

**STEPHEN**
"நீங்கதான் முதல்ல பார்த்தீங்களா?"

Meera nods.

**MEERA**
"ஆமா. வழக்கம்போல காலையில வந்தேன். சார் பதில் சொல்லல."

Stephen listens without interrupting.

**STEPHEN**
"கடைசியாக அவர் நார்மலா இருந்தது எப்போ?"

Meera describes Nagarajan's normal morning routine and the previous evening without speculating about what happened.

Across the room, Arjun watches.

Stephen notices him.

**STEPHEN**
"நீங்க?"

**ARJUN**
"Arjun. அவரோட பேரன்."

Stephen asks about Nagarajan's routine, medications, visitors and whether anyone had reason to enter the study that morning.

Arjun answers carefully.

A forensic officer approaches Stephen and quietly indicates an item found near Nagarajan.

Stephen examines it without touching it directly.

He then looks toward the study window.

The window is closed, but the latch appears slightly displaced.

Stephen walks over.

He studies the latch, the frame and the dust around it.

**STEPHEN**
"இந்த ஜன்னல்... காலையில யாராவது திறந்தாங்களா?"

Meera looks toward the window.

**MEERA**
"இல்லை சார். நான் உள்ளே வந்தப்போ இப்படித்தான் இருந்தது."

Stephen says nothing.

He looks once more at the latch.

Then at Arjun.

Then back toward Nagarajan's desk.

**STEPHEN**
"இது சாதாரணமான கேஸ்னு இப்பவே சொல்ல முடியாது."

The room falls quiet.

Stephen signals the forensic team to continue.

The camera holds on the slightly displaced window latch.

CUT TO BLACK.

## Mystery function
- Establishes Inspector Stephen's investigation method.
- Records Meera as the first discoverer/witness.
- Introduces Nagarajan's routine as a baseline for later contradictions.
- Establishes the window latch as an investigation clue without explaining it.
- Keeps every major suspect's motive unresolved.
