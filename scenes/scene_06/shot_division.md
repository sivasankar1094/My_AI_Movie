# Scene 06 — Shot Division

## 06A — Crime scene secured
Wide 35mm. Stephen enters and takes control. Meera stands aside; forensic staff work quietly.

## 06B — Stephen observes
85mm close-up. Stephen studies Nagarajan, desk and room before speaking.

## 06C — Meera statement
50mm medium close-up. Meera gives her account; restrained early-20s professional caregiver appearance, tied hair, semi-formal attire, no ID card.

## 06D — Arjun introduced
85mm close-up on Arjun listening. Stephen asks his relationship to Nagarajan.

## 06E — Routine notes
50mm insert of a notebook/daily routine record. Stephen reviews wake-up, tea, walk and morning habits as described by Meera/household.

## 06F — Forensic work
35mm controlled wide/medium shots of gloved forensic officers documenting and collecting evidence.

## 06G — Stephen's question
50mm two-shot. Stephen asks whether anyone entered the study that morning.

## 06H — Window latch
85mm macro insert. Slightly displaced window latch and frame/dust detail.

## 06I — Growing doubt
85mm close-up. Stephen considers the inconsistency; no exaggerated reaction.

### Estimated scene duration
