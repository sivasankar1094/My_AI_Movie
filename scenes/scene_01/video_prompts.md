# Scene 01 — Video Generation Prompts

## Global prompt
Photorealistic premium Tamil-cinema investigation thriller. Grand but believable Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury estate belonging to a veteran superstar and producer. Bright natural morning daylight, realistic South Indian architecture and materials, subtle mystery, natural atmospheric movement, restrained cinematic camera work, realistic physics, realistic glass reflections, natural wind, authentic scale. Preserve the exact approved estate design across every shot.

## Shot 01A
Slow cinematic approach toward the estate in bright morning daylight. Camera on a controlled 24mm dolly/steadicam movement. Natural breeze moves trees and subtle exterior fabric. Architecture remains fixed. No dramatic fog, no gothic styling, no supernatural elements.

## Shot 01B
Controlled 35–50mm movement through celebration remnants. Tasteful decorations and furniture remain naturally placed. A modern drinks glass catches morning light. It must clearly look like a drinks glass, not a tea tumbler. Natural reflections and physically correct glass movement only.

## Shot 01C
Quiet environmental hold on the estate. Very subtle movement in leaves, curtains and distant environmental elements. Camera remains stable. The contrast between recent celebration remnants and current silence creates tension.

## Shot 01D
Controlled 50–85mm move toward the upper-floor slightly open window. Curtain moves gently in the morning breeze. Bright daylight remains consistent. End on the window as an observational mystery hook.

## Negative prompt
No gothic European mansion, no supernatural horror, no exaggerated fog, no lightning, no cartoon look, no face morphing, no random architecture changes, no impossible camera movement, no floating objects, no duplicated props, no tea tumbler, no Western suburban setting, no artificial dramatic lighting.
