# Scene 01 — Shot Division

## Shot 01A — Estate establishing
- Wide 24mm cinematic exterior.
- Slow controlled forward movement toward estate.
- Bright natural morning light.
- Establish Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury architecture.

## Shot 01B — Celebration remnants
- 35–50mm controlled tracking/detail sequence.
- Tasteful decorations, chairs, table and modern drinks glass.
- Modern drinks glass must not resemble a tea tumbler.

## Shot 01C — Silent estate
- Medium/wide environmental shot.
- Hold long enough to communicate that the house is unusually quiet.
- Natural birds, breeze and distant environmental ambience.

## Shot 01D — Upper window
- 50–85mm controlled push or lateral move.
- Slightly open upper window.
- Curtain moving gently in morning breeze.
- This is the principal mystery hook.

## Editorial intent
The opening should be restrained and observational. Do not use a loud horror sting. The mystery should emerge from the contrast between a grand house, celebration remnants and unnatural quiet.

## Continuity lock
Use the approved Nagarajan Estate visual reference for all Scene 01 shots and future exterior continuity.
