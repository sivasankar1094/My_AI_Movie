# Scene 01 — Voice / Sound / Music

## Voice
No principal dialogue required in the current opening visual direction.

## Sound design
- Early morning birds.
- Light breeze through trees and exterior fabrics.
- Very subtle distant environmental/estate ambience.
- No exaggerated horror effects.
- Silence should become increasingly noticeable as the camera approaches the upper window.

## Music
Use a restrained original investigation-thriller texture. Begin minimally or delay music until the final upper-window image. Avoid a loud horror sting.

## Mix intent
Naturalistic cinematic mix. Environmental sound should establish physical space. Music must never overwhelm the natural atmosphere.
