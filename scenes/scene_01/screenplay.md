# Scene 01 — Screenplay / Current Approved Visual Direction

## EXT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — MORNING

A grand Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury estate sits in bright natural morning light. The architecture is elegant, substantial and believable for a veteran superstar and producer who built a major entertainment empire.

The previous night's celebration has left small traces across the property: tasteful decorations, chairs, a table, and drinking glasses. The house is quiet despite the evidence of a recent gathering.

The camera approaches slowly. Morning birds and a light breeze create a calm natural soundscape. The upper floor becomes visible. One window is slightly open; its curtain moves gently in the morning air.

The visual question is established without dialogue: something happened here.

## Visual direction
- Bright natural morning daylight.
- Tamil Nadu-inspired architecture.
- Grand but believable luxury.
- Peaceful, elegant, subtly mysterious.
- Photorealistic premium Tamil-cinema look.
- No gothic mansion styling, supernatural imagery or exaggerated horror treatment.
