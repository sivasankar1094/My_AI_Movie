# Scene 07 — Audio Production Plan

## Recording stems
1. Stephen dialogue
2. Meera dialogue
3. Arjun dialogue
4. Forensic/officer incidental voices
5. Estate room tone
6. CCTV/interface SFX
7. Paper/notebook/pen Foley
8. Footsteps and door ambience
9. Investigative music

## Dialogue capture
Record clean isolated Tamil dialogue with at least two restrained takes per line. Maintain the same Stephen, Meera and Arjun voices established in earlier scenes. Do not imitate a recognizable real actor.

## SFX synchronization
- CCTV pause/replay clicks align exactly with 07A–07B.
- Notebook page turns and pen movement align with 07F.
- Evidence-bag handling aligns with 07E.
- Garden-door environmental change begins as the camera turns toward the garden in 07I.

## Dynamic range
Keep the investigation quiet and intimate. The unidentified CCTV figure should create tension through a subtle reduction in ambience and a restrained tonal cue, not a loud effect.

## Audio QC
- Speech intelligible at low playback volume
- No clipping or distortion
- No voice identity drift
- No music masking dialogue
- Natural estate ambience
- No horror-style stingers
- Clean transition into Scene 08
