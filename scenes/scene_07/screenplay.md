# Scene 07 — Evidence & Leads

**Status:** READY FOR USER REVIEW
**Type:** Original Tamil investigation-thriller adaptation scene

## Scene purpose
Stephen moves from the physical examination of the study to the first structured evidence review. CCTV, Meera's timeline, Arjun's statement, Nagarajan's visitor record and a preliminary phone examination create several leads without identifying a killer. A small note from Nagarajan's desk points the investigation toward the garden side of the estate.

## Location
Nagarajan Estate — main hall / investigation table, CCTV monitor area, private study and estate gate/exterior.

## Time
Same morning, shortly after Scene 06.

## Continuity
Use the approved Nagarajan Estate geography and bright morning daylight. Meera is early 20s, freelance nurse/caregiver, tied hair, neat semi-formal attire, no ID card. Stephen remains calm and procedural. Arjun's approved appearance remains unchanged.

## Screenplay

**INT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — MAIN HALL — MORNING**

A temporary police investigation table has been set up in one corner of the main hall.

Inspector Stephen stands before a CCTV monitor. A forensic officer scrolls through the previous night's and early-morning footage.

Stephen watches without speaking.

A camera shows the side gate.

A car passes outside.

Then, earlier than expected, a figure crosses the edge of the frame.

Stephen pauses the footage.

**STEPHEN**
"இதை கொஞ்சம் பின்னாடி போங்க."

The footage rolls back.

The figure appears again, but only partially. The face cannot be identified.

Stephen makes a note.

Across the room, Meera sits with her hands together, trying to reconstruct the morning accurately.

**STEPHEN**
"நீங்க தினமும் சார் ரூமுக்கு போற நேரம்?"

**MEERA**
"சாதாரணமா எட்டு பதினைந்து... எட்டு இருபது."

**STEPHEN**
"இன்னைக்கு?"

**MEERA**
"எட்டு இருபத்தொன்னு இருக்கும்."

Stephen writes it down.

He turns to Arjun.

**STEPHEN**
"நீங்க காலையில எப்போ எழுந்தீங்க?"

Arjun gives his account carefully.

Stephen listens without accusing him.

A forensic officer brings Nagarajan's phone in an evidence bag.

Stephen looks at the screen and the call records.

One recent entry is incomplete because the number is unavailable.

Stephen looks up.

**STEPHEN**
"இந்த call யாருடையது?"

Arjun's face changes for only a moment.

**ARJUN**
"எனக்குத் தெரியாது."

Stephen studies him but does not press the point yet.

A household notebook is placed on the table.

It contains recent visitor entries.

Stephen turns the pages.

Milk supplier.

Garden worker.

Maintenance worker.

Then one handwritten entry has no name — only a time: **2:30 PM**.

Stephen underlines it.

**STEPHEN**
"இதுக்கு பேரே இல்லையா?"

Meera looks at the page.

**MEERA**
"நான் எழுதல சார்."

Stephen closes the notebook.

He returns to Nagarajan's desk and notices a small handwritten note among the papers.

Only a few words are visible:

**"Check garden side — 6:00 AM?"**

Stephen reads it twice.

He looks toward the estate garden through the main hall windows.

**STEPHEN**
"கார்டன் பக்கம் யாராவது போயிருக்காங்களா?"

No immediate answer.

Stephen signals one officer toward the garden entrance.

The camera moves toward the bright garden beyond the glass doors.

The estate remains beautiful and quiet.

CUT TO BLACK.

## End beat
The investigation gains multiple leads: an unidentified CCTV figure, an unavailable call record, an unexplained visitor entry and a note pointing toward the garden. None is yet identified as the murder solution.

## Mystery controls
- Do not reveal the CCTV person's identity.
- Do not establish the unavailable call as belonging to any suspect.
- Do not explain the 2:30 PM visitor entry.
- Do not reveal what Nagarajan meant by the garden note.
- Preserve all four as future investigation threads.
