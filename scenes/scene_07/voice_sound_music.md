## Voice direction
Stephen: low, measured, experienced and procedural. Meera: early-20s, professional, slightly anxious but precise. Arjun: restrained, guarded and natural; do not make him sound guilty. CCTV/forensic personnel remain mostly non-verbal.

## SFX / Foley
- CCTV playback button and interface clicks
- Paper and notebook page turns
- Pen writing and underline stroke
- Phone/evidence-bag handling
- Soft footsteps on polished estate flooring
- Distant household ambience
- Subtle chair movement
- Garden-door/room air movement

## Ambience
Bright morning estate ambience remains present. Birds and distant outdoor activity should be audible around the garden-facing shots. Indoors, maintain a quiet spacious room tone.

## Music
Begin with the restrained investigative motif from Scene 06. Add a very subtle pulse when the unidentified CCTV figure is paused. Reduce music during Meera's and Arjun's statements. Introduce a second restrained tonal layer at the visitor-log discovery. The garden note receives a quiet unresolved accent, not a horror sting.

## Mixing
Prioritize speech. CCTV and paper sounds should feel physical but not distract. Preserve pauses after Stephen asks questions. Avoid excessive reverb or bass. End with a clean transition bed for the next scene.
