# Scene 08 — Shot Division

## 08A — Entering the garden
Wide 35mm tracking shot. Stephen leads Meera and Arjun into the bright estate garden. Maintain approved estate architecture and morning light.

## 08B — Disturbed soil
85mm macro/insert. Stephen notices a small patch of freshly disturbed soil near the hedge. Forensic officer prepares camera.

## 08C — Meera observes
50mm medium close-up. Meera studies the soil, uncertain but attentive. Preserve early-20s appearance, tied hair and semi-formal attire.

## 08D — Footprints
Low 50mm shot of incomplete footprints in soft soil. Arjun notices them and calls Stephen's attention.

## 08E — Broken metal fragment
85mm close-up. Stephen, wearing gloves, lifts a small broken metal piece. Keep exact shape stable for later scenes.

## 08F — Side gate
35mm controlled pan from Stephen to the side gate. Fresh scratch marks are visible on the latch area.

## 08G — Stephen's theory
85mm close-up on Stephen. He looks from gate to house, forming a preliminary theory without declaring guilt.

## 08H — Garden worker
50mm two-shot of Stephen questioning the garden worker. Worker is nervous but not portrayed as definitively deceptive.

## 08I — Torn fabric
85mm macro. Small dark fabric fragment caught on a thorn. Forensic officer collects it carefully.

## 08J — Evidence bag
85mm insert. Metal/fabric evidence is secured and labelled. Avoid readable invented case numbers.

## 08K — Final geography
35mm slow pullback from evidence to side gate and then estate. End on unanswered questions.

### Performance
Stephen: restrained, procedural. Meera: observant, concerned, professional. Arjun: attentive and unsettled. Garden worker: nervous but ambiguous.

### Continuity
Keep garden hedge, side gate, latch, evidence positions and house geometry consistent for future investigation scenes.
