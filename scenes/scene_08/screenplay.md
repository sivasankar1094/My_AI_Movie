# Scene 08 — The Garden Clue

**Status:** READY FOR USER REVIEW
**Type:** Original Tamil investigation-thriller adaptation scene

## Scene purpose
Continue directly from Scene 07 as Stephen follows Nagarajan's note pointing toward the garden. The investigation uncovers physical traces suggesting recent movement near the side gate, while deliberately avoiding a definitive identification of the person involved.

## Location
Nagarajan Estate — garden, side hedge and side gate.

## Time
Same morning, shortly after Scene 07.

## Screenplay

**EXT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — GARDEN — MORNING**

Stephen walks toward the garden with Meera and Arjun following at a measured distance.

The garden is bright, peaceful and meticulously maintained. That calmness makes the small disturbances easier to notice.

Stephen stops near the hedge.

A patch of soil looks recently disturbed.

He crouches without touching it.

**STEPHEN**
"இது நேத்து இப்படி இருந்ததா?"

Meera looks carefully.

**MEERA**
"நான் கவனிக்கல சார்."

A forensic officer photographs the area.

A few steps away, Arjun notices faint footprints in the softer soil.

**ARJUN**
"சார்... இங்க பாருங்க."

Stephen moves over.

The prints are incomplete, but clearly recent.

Near them lies a small broken piece of metal.

Stephen examines it with gloved hands.

He looks toward the side gate.

The gate latch has fresh scratch marks.

Stephen's expression changes subtly.

**STEPHEN**
"யாராவது இந்த வழியா வந்திருக்கலாம்."

He looks back at the house.

The garden worker approaches nervously.

**STEPHEN**
"காலையில ஏதாவது வித்தியாசமா பார்த்தீங்களா?"

The worker shakes his head.

**GARDEN WORKER**
"இல்ல சார்... நான் ஆறு மணிக்குத்தான் வந்தேன். அதுக்கு முன்னாடி யாரும் தெரியல."

Stephen studies him, then looks again at the ground.

A small torn piece of dark fabric is caught on a thorn near the hedge.

The forensic officer carefully collects it into an evidence bag.

Stephen does not announce a conclusion.

He simply looks toward the side gate.

**STEPHEN**
"இங்க நடந்தது என்னன்னு கண்டுபிடிக்கணும்."

Meera watches silently.

Arjun looks toward the house.

The camera slowly moves from the evidence bag to the gate and then back toward the estate.

CUT TO BLACK.

## End beat
The garden provides a new physical investigation trail: disturbed soil, incomplete footprints, a broken metal fragment, fresh gate scratches and torn fabric. Their significance remains unresolved.

## Mystery controls
- Do not identify the footprint owner.
- Do not identify the fabric owner.
- Do not confirm that the metal fragment belongs to the gate.
- Do not establish that the garden worker is truthful or deceptive.
- Preserve all evidence for later forensic development.
