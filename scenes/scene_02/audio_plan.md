# Scene 02 — Audio, Dialogue, Sound & Music Plan

## Audio direction
Scene 02 should feel like a grounded Tamil investigation thriller. The morning world is bright and natural, while the phone call introduces the first controlled disturbance. Avoid loud horror-style effects or melodramatic scoring.

## Dialogue / voice
### Arjun
- Delivery: restrained, natural Tamil, low-to-medium volume.
- Emotional progression: routine morning → mild irritation → attention → concern → suspicion.
- Do not over-act the phone conversation.

### Unknown caller
- Voice: adult, gender intentionally neutral/unclear until story requires identification.
- Delivery: calm, deliberate and controlled.
- Keep the caller's identity hidden through vocal performance; no distinctive catchphrase or exaggerated villain voice.
- Dialogue should be recorded as clean dry dialogue first, with room tone added separately.

## Suggested dialogue beats
1. Phone rings/vibrates; Arjun answers.
2. Arjun: "ஹலோ?"
3. Caller gives a short identification-free response and asks whether Arjun is alone.
4. Arjun becomes alert: "யார் பேசுறது?"
5. Caller references Nagarajan and says there is something Arjun needs to know.
6. Arjun asks for clarification; caller avoids giving a full explanation.
7. Caller gives a private-meeting instruction and ends the call.

Keep wording concise. The exact final dialogue must be checked against the locked screenplay before voice generation.

## Sound effects by shot
- 02A: Morning birds, distant estate ambience, light leaves/wind, very subtle environmental bed.
- 02B: Room tone, ceiling/air-conditioning ambience if appropriate, bedsheet movement, breathing.
- 02C: Smartphone vibration/ringtone close and realistic; bedside-object resonance.
- 02D: Phone continuing in background, subtle breathing/cloth movement.
- 02E: Phone pickup, touch/screen sound, dialogue begins; reduce ringtone immediately.
- 02F: Very subtle low-frequency tension texture may enter after Nagarajan is mentioned.
- 02G: Balcony door handle and sliding-door sound; exterior ambience expands naturally when door opens.
- 02H: Open-air morning ambience, birds, distant estate sounds.
- 02I: Very faint environmental ambience; avoid obvious stingers when silhouette appears.
- 02J: Reduce ambience slightly to focus attention on Arjun's reaction.
- 02K: Return to full estate ambience, then controlled fade toward silence/cut.

## Music
- Opening: no conventional melody; use natural ambience.
- Phone call: introduce a very restrained investigative pulse/texture only after the call becomes suspicious.
- Silhouette reveal: one subtle tonal change rather than a jump scare.
- Ending: unresolved low sustained note or minimal two/three-note motif that can carry into Scene 03.

## Mixing
- Dialogue must remain clearly intelligible above music and ambience.
- Phone/ringtone should be prominent only before answering.
- Do not use excessive bass, reverb or compression.
- Exterior estate ambience should sound open and realistic.
- Preserve dynamic range so the quiet moments remain quiet.

## Voice-generation continuity
Keep Arjun's voice identical across scenes. Keep the unknown caller's voice identical whenever the caller reappears. Do not synthesize a voice that sounds like a recognizable real actor.

## Final audio deliverables
- Dialogue stem
- Caller voice stem
- Foley/SFX stem
- Atmosphere/room-tone stem
- Music stem
- Final stereo mix
- Optional 5.1 mix when the final edit workflow supports it
