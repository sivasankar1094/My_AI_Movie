# Scene 02 — An Unexpected Call

**Status:** Awaiting user review  
**Type:** Original Tamil investigation-thriller adaptation scene

## Scene purpose
Shift the film from the peaceful morning established in Scene 01 into the first personal mystery. Arjun receives an unexpected call connected to Nagarajan. The scene introduces a question without revealing the answer.

## Location
Arjun's guest bedroom / private suite in Nagarajan Estate.

## Time
Morning, approximately 8:10 AM, shortly after the estate establishing sequence.

## Visual continuity
Use the approved Nagarajan Estate design from Scene 01: grand Tamil Nadu-inspired luxury estate, believable South Indian architecture, bright natural morning daylight. Keep the same architecture and environmental design.

## Screenplay

**INT. ARJUN'S ROOM — MORNING**

Bright sunlight passes through tall curtains. The room is quiet. The distant sounds of household activity suggest that the estate has begun its normal morning routine.

Arjun lies awake, looking at the ceiling. He is not fully relaxed. His phone rests beside him.

The phone vibrates.

Unknown number.

Arjun looks at the screen but does not answer immediately.

It rings again.

He answers.

**ARJUN**
"ஹலோ?"

Silence.

A faint breath on the other end.

**UNKNOWN CALLER**
"நீங்க என்னைத் தெரியாது. ஆனா... உங்களுக்கு தெரிஞ்சிருக்க வேண்டிய ஒரு விஷயம் இருக்கு."

Arjun sits up.

**ARJUN**
"யார் பேசுறது?"

The caller does not answer directly.

**UNKNOWN CALLER**
"உங்க தாத்தாவைப் பற்றி."

Arjun's expression changes slightly.

**ARJUN**
"என்ன விஷயம்?"

A pause.

**UNKNOWN CALLER**
"இதை phone-ல சொல்ல முடியாது."

**ARJUN**
"அப்படின்னா நேர்ல சொல்லுங்க."

**UNKNOWN CALLER**
"இன்று மாலை. நீங்க மட்டும் வாங்க."

The call ends.

Arjun stares at the phone.

He checks the number.

Private / unavailable.

He walks toward the balcony doors and opens them.

The bright estate is visible below.

From this height, the house looks peaceful and perfect.

Arjun looks toward Nagarajan's wing.

For a moment, he notices a figure moving behind an upstairs curtain.

He cannot identify who it is.

He keeps watching.

CUT TO BLACK.

## End beat
The audience understands that someone outside the family knows something about Nagarajan, but neither Arjun nor the audience knows whether the caller is a witness, a manipulator, or a trap.
