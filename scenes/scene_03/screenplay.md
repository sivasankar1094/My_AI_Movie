# Scene 03 — The Morning Routine

**Status:** Ready for user review
**Type:** Original Tamil investigation-thriller adaptation scene

## Scene purpose
Follow the morning household routine and introduce Meera as Nagarajan's trusted freelance nurse/caregiver. The scene creates the first anomaly: Nagarajan's normal morning routine has been disturbed. It should build unease without immediately revealing the death.

## Location
Nagarajan Estate — upper-floor corridor and Nagarajan's private bedroom.

## Time
Morning, approximately 8:20 AM.

## Continuity
Bright natural morning daylight. Use the approved Tamil Nadu-inspired Nagarajan Estate architecture from Scene 01. Meera must always keep her hair tied and wear semi-formal caregiver attire, without an ID card. She is a freelance nurse, not household staff.

## Screenplay

**INT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — UPPER CORRIDOR — MORNING**

The estate is fully awake now.

Servants move quietly in the distant parts of the house. A newspaper lands near the main entrance. A ceiling fan turns slowly in the corridor.

Meera walks upstairs carrying a compact medical pouch and a glass of warm water.

Her hair is neatly tied. She wears a simple, well-fitted semi-formal kurta with trousers. No uniform. No ID card.

She reaches Nagarajan's bedroom door.

She knocks once.

**MEERA**
"சார்... காலை மருந்து நேரம்."

No response.

She smiles faintly, assuming he is asleep.

She knocks again.

**MEERA**
"சார்?"

Still nothing.

Meera checks her watch.

8:21 AM.

She gently opens the door.

**INT. NAGARAJAN'S BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS**

Bright morning light fills the room.

The bed is neatly made.

Too neatly.

Nagarajan normally leaves his reading glasses and morning book beside the bed. Today the glasses are on the side table, but the book is closed.

Meera notices the untouched water glass from the previous night.

She looks toward the bathroom door.

Closed.

She walks closer.

**MEERA**
"சார்?"

She opens the bathroom door.

Empty.

Her expression changes.

She turns toward the private study entrance.

The study door is slightly open.

Meera stops.

She notices something on the floor just inside the study — a small metallic object.

She bends slightly but does not touch it.

A faint sense of alarm appears on her face.

She calls out once more.

**MEERA**
"சார்... நான் உள்ள வர்றேன்."

She pushes the door open.

We do not see what she sees.

Instead, stay on Meera's face.

Her breathing stops for a moment.

The glass of water slips slightly in her hand.

CUT TO BLACK.

## End beat
The audience understands that Meera has discovered something serious inside the study, but the body/reveal is withheld for the following scene.

## Mystery function
- Establish Nagarajan's precise morning routine.
- Establish Meera's privileged access and professional role.
- Introduce an unexplained metallic object as a visual clue.
- Delay the death reveal by one scene.
- Preserve the unknown caller from Scene 02 as an unresolved thread.
