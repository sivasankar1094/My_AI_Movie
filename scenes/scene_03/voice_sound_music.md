# Scene 03 — Voice, Sound & Music

## Dialogue
MEERA: "சார்... காலை மருந்து நேரம்."
MEERA: "சார்?"
MEERA: "சார்... நான் உள்ள வர்றேன்."

## Voice direction
Meera: adult Tamil female voice, professional caregiver, warm but restrained. Begin routine and calm; shift gradually to concern and shock. Avoid melodrama.

## Foley
- Soft footsteps on polished floor
- Medical pouch movement
- Glass of warm water gently shifting in hand
- Two soft knocks on wooden door
- Door handle movement
- Bathroom door opening
- Very subtle fabric movement
- Door to study opening

## Ambience
Bright morning estate ambience: distant household activity, birds outside, ceiling fan, faint kitchen sounds, natural room tone.

## Music
Start with no score. Introduce an almost imperceptible low suspense texture when Meera notices the untouched water glass. Increase slightly at the study door. Cut the score completely or nearly completely at the moment she freezes, allowing room tone and breathing to carry the beat.

## Mix direction
Dialogue clear and intimate. Environmental sounds remain natural and never overpower dialogue. The metallic-object insert should have a subtle realistic contact sound only if Meera moves near it; do not make it sound like a horror-film sting.
