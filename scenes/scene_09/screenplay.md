# Scene 09 — Pieces Come Together

**Status:** READY FOR USER REVIEW
**Type:** Original Tamil investigation-thriller adaptation scene

## Scene purpose
Continue directly from Scene 08. Stephen begins connecting the garden evidence with Nagarajan's private notes and the estate's CCTV. The scene increases suspicion without identifying the perpetrator.

## Location
Nagarajan Estate — study / evidence table / garden.

## Time
Same morning.

## Screenplay

Stephen places the sealed fabric fragment and metal piece beside the photographs from the garden. He studies them without touching the evidence directly.

MEERA watches from a short distance.

STEPHEN
"இந்த துணி... தோட்டத்துல கிடைச்சது மட்டும் இல்ல. யாரோ அவசரமா போயிருக்காங்க."

ARJUN
"அப்படின்னா அந்த ஆள் வீட்டுக்குள்ள வந்திருக்கணும்."

Stephen does not answer immediately.

He opens Nagarajan's personal notebook. One recent page contains short reminders about the side gate, a contractor visit and an unexplained early-morning entry.

A CCTV operator brings up footage from the side gate. The recording shows an indistinct figure moving near the gate during the relevant window, but the face cannot be identified.

STEPHEN
"Zoom பண்ணுங்க."

The image enlarges. Still no usable face.

The garden worker is brought in again.

STEPHEN
"நீங்க ஆறு மணிக்கு வந்தீங்கன்னு சொன்னீங்க. அதுக்கு முன்னாடி gate யாராவது use பண்ணினாங்களா?"

The worker hesitates.

GARDEN WORKER
"எனக்குத் தெரியாது சார். நான் வந்தப்போ latch கொஞ்சம் loose-ஆ இருந்தது."

Stephen exchanges a look with Arjun.

Meera remembers something.

MEERA
"சார்... நேத்து இரவு சார் ஒரு call காக வெளியே போகணும்னு சொன்னது ஞாபகம் இருக்கு."

Stephen turns to her.

STEPHEN
"எந்த நேரம்?"

Meera thinks.

MEERA
"சரியா தெரியல... ஆனா ரொம்ப late இல்ல."

Stephen writes it down.

The camera returns to the evidence board: fabric, metal fragment, gate photograph, CCTV still and Nagarajan's notebook.

Stephen connects two points with a marker.

STEPHEN
"இது தனித்தனி விஷயம் மாதிரி தெரியல."

He looks toward the house.

STEPHEN
"யாரோ ஏதோ மறைக்கிறாங்க."

CUT TO BLACK.

## End beat
The investigation now has a connected physical trail, but the identity and motive remain unresolved.

## Mystery controls
- CCTV figure remains unidentified.
- Fabric ownership remains unknown.
- Metal fragment identity remains unconfirmed.
- Garden worker is not declared guilty or innocent.
- Meera's memory is treated as a lead, not proof.
