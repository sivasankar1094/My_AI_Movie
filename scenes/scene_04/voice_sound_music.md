# Scene 04 — Voice, Sound & Music Plan

## Dialogue
MEERA: "சார்...?"
MEERA: "யாராவது மேல வாங்க! சீக்கிரம்!"

## Voice direction
Meera is a freelance nurse/caregiver. Her voice begins controlled and professional, then develops into contained shock. The call for help should sound instinctive and urgent but not theatrical.

## Foley / SFX
- Study door hinge/handle movement
- Soft footsteps entering the study
- Cloth movement from Meera's semi-formal attire
- Glass placed carefully on a surface
- Subtle phone handling/touch sound
- Meera's breath becoming audible
- Distant footsteps responding to her call
- Natural room acoustics

## Ambience
Bright morning estate ambience continues: birds, distant household activity, ceiling fan/air movement and faint exterior sounds. Once Meera sees Nagarajan, reduce competing ambience slightly to focus the audience on her breathing and the room's stillness.

## Music
Start with the restrained suspense texture carried from Scene 03. At the first visual confirmation of Nagarajan, introduce a low sustained investigative tone rather than a horror sting. Allow the music to recede during Meera's call for help. End with a short unresolved tonal bed leading into the next scene.

## Mixing
Dialogue must remain intelligible. Do not over-compress Meera's voice. Preserve the contrast between the quiet study and her sudden call for help. Avoid exaggerated cinematic impact sounds for the body discovery.

## Voice continuity
Use the same approved Meera voice used in Scene 03. No recognizable real-actor voice imitation.
