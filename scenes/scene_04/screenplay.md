## Screenplay

**INT. NAGARAJAN ESTATE — PRIVATE STUDY — MORNING**

The study door opens slowly.

Meera stands at the threshold.

For one second, she cannot move.

Her eyes settle on Nagarajan.

He is motionless beside his desk.

The room is otherwise unnaturally still.

The morning sunlight entering through the windows makes the scene feel disturbingly ordinary rather than theatrical.

Meera takes one cautious step inside.

The glass of water trembles in her hand.

She places it on a nearby surface without taking her eyes off Nagarajan.

**MEERA**
"சார்...?"

No response.

She moves closer, stopping before touching him.

Her professional instincts briefly overcome her fear. She checks for a response and then realizes that something is seriously wrong.

Her breathing becomes shallow.

She looks toward the desk.

The small metallic object seen in Scene 03 remains on the floor near the study entrance.

Meera looks from the object to Nagarajan and back again.

She reaches for her phone.

Before making the call, she hesitates for a fraction of a second.

Her eyes move across the room as though trying to understand what happened.

She calls for help.

**MEERA**
"யாராவது மேல வாங்க! சீக்கிரம்!"

Her voice carries through the otherwise quiet estate.

Distant footsteps begin.

Meera remains beside the desk, shaken but trying to stay composed.

The camera slowly widens, revealing the study, Nagarajan and the surrounding objects without prematurely explaining the cause of death.

CUT TO BLACK.
