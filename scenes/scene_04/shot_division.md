# Scene 04 — Shot Division

## 04A — Door opening
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: Tight over-shoulder / doorway
- Lens: 50mm
- Action: Meera pushes the study door open from the exact Scene 03 endpoint.
- Continuity: Same costume, tied hair, pouch and water glass.

## 04B — Meera freezes
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: Tight close-up
- Lens: 85mm
- Action: Her eyes react before the audience is shown the full discovery.

## 04C — First reveal
- Duration: 6s
- Shot: Medium-wide study interior
- Lens: 35mm
- Action: Nagarajan is visible, motionless beside/at his desk. Keep the death non-graphic and unexplained.

## 04D — Meera's shock
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: 85mm close-up
- Action: Meera takes a breath, tries to remain professional.

## 04E — Water glass
- Duration: 3s
- Shot: Insert
- Lens: 85mm
- Action: Her trembling hand places the glass down.

## 04F — Calling him
- Duration: 4s
- Shot: Medium close-up
- Action: Meera quietly says, "சார்...?" No response.

## 04G — Professional instinct
- Duration: 6s
- Shot: Medium
- Lens: 50mm
- Action: Meera cautiously checks for a response without graphic detail.

## 04H — Desk and room
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: Controlled detail sequence
- Lens: 50–85mm
- Action: Desk, personal objects and room geometry establish the study as an investigation location.

## 04I — Metallic clue
- Duration: 4s
- Shot: Low insert
- Lens: 85mm
- Action: The same small metallic object from Scene 03 is visible near the entrance. Meera notices it but does not touch it.

## 04J — Decision to call
- Duration: 4s
- Shot: Close-up on phone/Meera
- Action: She takes out her phone, hesitates briefly, then calls for help.

## 04K — Cry for help
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: Medium close-up
- Action: "யாராவது மேல வாங்க! சீக்கிரம்!" Her voice travels through the estate.

## 04L — Footsteps approach
- Duration: 5s
- Shot: Wide study/doorway
- Action: Distant footsteps begin approaching. Meera remains beside Nagarajan.

## 04M — Final reveal / transition
- Duration: 6s
- Shot: Slow controlled widening
- Action: Study, Nagarajan and Meera share the frame. Hold on unanswered questions, then cut to black.

### Camera geography
The study entrance, desk, windows and recurring objects must retain the same spatial relationship in all future investigation shots.

### Performance
Meera's reaction is restrained and realistic. Shock is communicated through breathing, eyes and posture rather than screaming or melodrama.
